> "$1,000/month covers complete campaign management — keyword research, ad copy testing, bid strategy, conversion tracking, and ongoing optimization. $1,000 one-time setup fee covers a full audit of your Google Ads account, landing page creation in GoHighLevel, and conversion tracking setup. For ad spends above $5,000/month, the management fee shifts to 20% of ad spend."

**Two packaging options:**
1. **$1,000/mo + $1,000 setup** — month-to-month, no contract.
2. **$1,000/mo with 6-month commitment** — setup fee waived.

**Monthly fee includes:** weekly campaign reviews, GoHighLevel landing pages hosted on DuoDigital's agency account (client saves ~$99/mo), ad copy/keyword refinements, conversion-tracking validation, monthly performance reports.

- Ad spend billed **directly to the client's card** in Google Ads — *"we do not collect any payments."*

## 4. Processes & Workflows

### Sales → Onboarding Flow
1. **Discovery call** → written recap email with pricing options.
2. **Simple agreement + first-month invoice** (QuickBooks link; Bill.com ACH accepted).
3. **Welcome email** — *"Let's get started! 🎉 …welcome you to the DuoDigital family."*
4. **Client onboarding tasks:**
   - Create Google Ads account.
   - Create CallRail account (basic Call Tracking plan).
   - Create LSA account + Evident verification.
   - Complete onboarding questionnaire.
   - Grant access (Meta: add shaquira@agilecodelab.com as Advertiser).
   - Point landing-page subdomain via client's GoDaddy.
5. **Kickoff call** — ad spend finalized here.
6. **Keyword research / ad copy review** with client → **launch.**

**Project-work billing:** 50% to start, 50% on delivery; hourly billed in 5/20/25-hour increments; time logs provided on request.

### Monthly Reporting Cadence (PPC/LSA clients)
- Email from Shaquira on ~1st business day + monthly Zoom/Meet review meeting.
- **Report format (verbatim example, Full Circle):**
  > "Google LSAs: 18 calls | $1,908 spend | $119 per lead — Google PPC: 14 calls + 7 form submissions (21 total leads) | $1,668 spend | $80 per lead. Compared to last month, your cost per lead decreased by $12 and your lead volume doubled… You can view the full report here: [AgencyAnalytics link]. This is an ongoing dashboard link, so it will continue to update each month."
- Plus month-over-month commentary, marketing highlights, and pre-meeting reminders.
- **Closed loop:** clients feed downstream revenue data back (consult → hire → case-value spreadsheets).
- **Lead notifications:** *"New Lead (form submission) from Google Ads — please follow up ASAP"* auto-forwarded from shaquira+…@mail.duodigital.io.

### Attribution Doctrine
- CallRail unique numbers per channel (GBP primary number swap; main line as 2nd for NAP consistency).
- Intake staff always ask *"how did you find us."*
